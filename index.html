A LITTLE WORLD BY JACK

For Quiet

There are things I could say in a thousand messages...
but somehow, I wanted to build you a little world instead.

SCROLL INTO MY HEART

THE GIRL I KNOW

Somehow, you became
my favorite world.

Maybe it's the little things. The things you probably don't even realize I notice.

☔

Rain

You love the rain. And somehow every rainy evening makes me imagine sitting beside you, saying absolutely nothing.

🐈

Cats

You and your cats. A tiny world of your own... and somehow I want a place inside it.

☁️

Your sleepy little world

You could sleep for half the day and still somehow make me smile. I don't know how you do it.

♡

Even your anger

I somehow like even that. Although I'll admit... sometimes your anger scares me.

AND THEN THERE'S ME

Your crazy stupid.

Yes... the crazy stupid one.

The one who wants to cook for you just because seeing you happy would be enough.

The one who would happily fall asleep on your lap and forget about the entire world.

The one who can't really stay away from you.

Maybe I'm a little addicted to your presence.

And honestly... I'm not even sorry.

THE ONE THING I DON'T SAY

I have a fear.

Sometimes I wonder...

What if one day you look at me and decide you don't want me anymore?

What if the person I can't stay away from becomes the person I have to learn to live without?

I probably don't say it.
But secretly... I'm always afraid of losing you.

MY PROMISE

I wanna be yours

until I live,
until I die.

Not because I want to own your world.

Because I want to be a part of it.

ONE DAY

Salma's Citadel

That's what I want to call our home.

One day, I want to build a home so beautiful that it feels like it was made only for you.

A place where you don't have to pretend, impress anyone, or become someone else.

You just get to be yourself.
My girl. My princess.

MY FAVORITE DREAM

One day...

I want to sit beside you
on a quiet terrace.

I want the city to sleep below us,
the rain to disappear into the night,
and the moon to be the only light
we really need.

No big plans.
No perfect words.

Just you, me...
and the moon.

— Jack
THE END? Maybe just the beginning.
