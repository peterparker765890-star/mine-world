A little something

For You

There are some things
that are easier to feel
than to say.

CHAPTER I

Sometimes,
someone simply
becomes special.

✦

Not because they tried to be. Not because they asked for it. Somehow, their smile, their voice, their presence... starts making ordinary moments feel a little more beautiful.

CHAPTER II

♡

And then
there is you.

Maybe I don't always know
the perfect words.

But I know how it feels
when I think about you.

A LETTER

Dear you,

If I could keep a collection of beautiful little moments, I think many of them would somehow have you in them.

I don't know what tomorrow looks like. I don't know where every road in life will take us.

But there is one little dream that I keep imagining...

— From someone who cares

MY LITTLE DREAM

One day...

I want to sit with you
on a quiet terrace,

watch the moon above us,
feel the night breeze,
and forget about everything
else for a while.

✦

No big plans.
No perfect words.

Just you, me...
and the moon.
